💻 macOS 端：辅助功能 (Accessibility) 授权设置指南

在 macOS 上，TypePrompt 拥有一键将选中的提示词直接粘贴到当前活跃应用（如 ChatGPT、Claude、浏览器、邮件等）的功能。为了能够模拟按键进行粘贴，TypePrompt 需要系统授予 **辅助功能 (Accessibility)** 权限。配置步骤如下：

  1. 点击 Mac 屏幕左上角的苹果图标 ，选择 系统设置 (System Settings)。
  2. 在左侧侧边栏中向下滚动，选择 隐私与安全性 (Privacy & Security)。
  3. 在右侧列表中找到并点击 辅助功能 (Accessibility)。
  4. 在应用列表中找到 TypePrompt，打开其右侧的开关以授予权限。
  5. 如果在列表中未找到 TypePrompt：请点击列表下方的 “+” 按钮，在弹出的文件选择器中，进入“应用程序 (Applications)”目录，选中 TypePrompt.app 并添加，然后再确保其开关已处于开启状态。

*注：我们极其重视您的隐私安全。TypePrompt 是一款 100% 离线的本地应用，我们不会收集、上传您的任何文本输入或个人隐私。申请该权限仅用于执行 CMD + V 智能模拟粘贴操作。

📱 iOS 端：键盘“完全访问 (Full Access)”权限说明

为了让您在 iOS 输入法键盘中能够读取主 App 数据库中自定义和导入的提示词，iOS 系统要求开启完全访问权限（用于在主 App 进程和键盘扩展进程之间共享 SwiftData 数据库）。

  1. 打开 iPhone 的 设置 (Settings)。
  2. 依次进入 通用 (General) -> 键盘 (Keyboard) -> 键盘 (Keyboards)。
  3. 点击列表中的 TypePrompt。
  4. 开启 “允许完全访问 (Allow Full Access)” 的开关。

🌐 跨端：App 专属界面语言切换指南

TypePrompt 完美支持 6 国语言（简体中文、English、日本語、Español、Français、Deutsch）。如果您希望单独更改本软件的界面语言，可以按以下方式设置：

💻 macOS: Accessibility Permission Setup Guide

On macOS, TypePrompt features a 1-tap paste tool that directly inserts your chosen prompt into the currently active application (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, browsers, email clients). To simulate the paste action, TypePrompt requests **Accessibility** permission. Follow these steps to set it up:

  1. Click the Apple menu  in the top-left corner of your Mac screen and choose System Settings.
  2. Scroll down in the left sidebar and select Privacy & Security.
  3. Locate and click Accessibility in the right-hand list.
  4. Find TypePrompt in the app list and toggle the switch to ON.
  5. If TypePrompt is not in the list: Click the "+" icon at the bottom of the list, navigate to your "Applications" folder, select TypePrompt.app to add it, and ensure its toggle is turned on.

*Note: We highly value your privacy. TypePrompt operates 100% offline and locally. We do not collect, monitor, or transmit any of your text inputs. This permission is strictly used to post simulated CMD + V keystroke events to automate pasting.

📱 iOS: Keyboard "Allow Full Access" Configuration

To access your custom prompts directly within the iOS custom system keyboard, iOS requires you to grant "Full Access" permission. This is used solely to share your local SwiftData database between the host application container and the keyboard extension container.

  1. Open the iOS Settings app.
  2. Navigate to General -> Keyboard -> Keyboards.
  3. Tap TypePrompt in the list.
  4. Toggle the switch to enable "Allow Full Access".

🌐 Per-App Language Settings Guide

TypePrompt natively supports 6 languages (Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German). If you want to change the app language independently from your system language, follow these steps:

  • iOS (iPhone): Open the system Settings app -> Scroll down and find TypePrompt in the app list -> Tap Preferred Language (Language) -> Choose your preferred language.
  • macOS (Mac): Click the Apple menu  -> Select System Settings -> Navigate to General -> Select Language & Region -> Scroll down to the Applications section and click the "+" button -> Choose TypePrompt and select your preferred language.
